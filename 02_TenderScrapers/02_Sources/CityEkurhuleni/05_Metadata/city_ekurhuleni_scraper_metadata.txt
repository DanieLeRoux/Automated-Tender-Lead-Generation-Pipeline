Detail-page pattern

https://www.ekurhuleni.gov.za/business/tenders/<tender-slug>/

Purpose
-------
Scrapes City of Ekurhuleni open tenders over R750 000 and enriches listing-page records by opening each tender detail page.

Scraper behaviour
-----------------
1. Fetches the City of Ekurhuleni Open Tenders listing page.
2. Detects tender detail links under /business/tenders/.
3. Probes numbered open-tender listing pages, such as /open-tenders/2/.
4. Opens each tender detail page.
5. Extracts bid number, full description, closing date/time, information-session details, contact details, submission instructions and document links.
6. Writes raw outputs, filters current tenders into the master, and calculates source-level delta/update/top-delta outputs.

Known limitations
-----------------
- The open-tenders listing page uses short descriptions; full details are primarily on each tender detail page.
- Pagination may change if the website structure is updated.
- The source-level master only retains current tenders with valid closing dates.
- The central consolidated daily review applies the standard exclusion and scope-fit filters, so operational ICT, supply, repairs, maintenance and similar tenders may be retained in the source master but excluded from the bid-manager review.
