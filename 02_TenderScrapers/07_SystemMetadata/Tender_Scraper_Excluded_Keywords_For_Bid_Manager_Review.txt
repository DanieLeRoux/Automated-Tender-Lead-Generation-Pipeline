TenderScrapers - Current Excluded Keyword / Theme List
=====================================================

Purpose
-------
These terms and themes are currently used to exclude tenders from the bid-manager daily review. Excluded records are still retained in the consolidated master register for audit and review.

Important Principle
-------------------
Priority clients should boost relevant tenders, but a priority client should not make an irrelevant tender appear in the daily review.

Caution / Review Notes
----------------------
Some terms should not be excluded too broadly:

- "plant" should not be excluded generally because water treatment plant may be relevant.
- "transport" should not be excluded generally because transport planning and BRT may be relevant.
- "design" should not be excluded generally because engineering design is relevant, although graphic design/publication design is excluded.
- "financial advisory" should be reviewed carefully because infrastructure transaction advisory may be relevant, while accounting/financial admin advisory may not be.
- "refurbishment" should not be excluded globally where the tender is for professional engineering services linked to infrastructure or treatment works.

Suggested Bid Manager Feedback
------------------------------
Please review whether any of the above exclusions are too broad or whether additional irrelevant categories should be added.
